Fit Analysis

Likely, Match, or Reach — Instantly

Scorgi compares the student's best SAT or ACT composite against each school's middle 50% range: Likely if they score at or above the 75th percentile, Match at or above the 25th, and Reach below the 25th.

  • Application Tracking

    Track every application through 8 statuses: Interested, Researching, Applying, Applied, Accepted, Waitlisted, Enrolled, and Withdrawn.

  • Pre-Built Checklists

    Each school comes with a pre-built checklist covering Common App, essays, recommendation letters, transcripts, test scores, FAFSA, and application fees.
