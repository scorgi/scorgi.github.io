Boards & Cards

Organize Work Your Way

Create customizable lists (columns) and drag-and-drop cards between them. Each card is a rich workspace with everything your team needs to stay aligned.

  • Rich Cards

    Every card supports a name, description, due dates, star priority, assigned members, a designated leader, color-coded labels, threaded comments, and nested checklists.

  • Drag-and-Drop

    Move cards between lists and reorder them within a list with smooth drag-and-drop. Changes sync instantly across all connected browsers.

  • Nested Checklists

    Break work into sub-tasks with nested checklists. Track completion percentage at a glance and never lose sight of what's left.

  • Labels & Priorities

    Color-coded labels categorize work by type, subject, or urgency. Star cards to mark high-priority items that need immediate attention.

Real-Time & Integrations

Collaborate Without Refreshing

WebSocket-powered real-time collaboration means every card move, comment, and status change appears instantly for all connected team members. No page refreshes needed.

  • List Subscribers

    Subscribe to any list and receive push notifications whenever cards are added, moved, or updated. Stay informed without watching the board all day.

  • Full Audit Trail

    Every action is logged with timestamps and user attribution. Review the complete history of any card, list, or board at any time.

  • Gantt Chart View

    Switch to Gantt view to visualize timelines and dependencies across cards. See how tasks connect and identify scheduling conflicts.

  • Slack Integration

    Connect to Slack for instant notifications and AI-generated Monday summaries that recap the week's progress across all boards.

  • Card Cloning

    Clone cards as templates for recurring tasks. Duplicate a card's structure — checklists, labels, assignments — in one click.
