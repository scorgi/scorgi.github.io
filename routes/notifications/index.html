Pick the Audience, Watch It Update

    Session-Based, Query, or Custom List — switched with one control at the top of the composer.

Dual-Channel Delivery

Email & SMS, Together

Toggle each channel independently — send email-only, SMS-only, or both. Emails use branded templates with your company logo and automatic unsubscribe links. SMS messages show a real-time character counter with segment count display.

  • Email

    Branded template with company logo, XSS-safe content, subject + body composition, and unsubscribe link.

  • SMS

    Twilio-powered with character counter, auto-segment splitting at 160 chars, and company name prefix.

  • AI Content Analysis

    One-click spam score (1-10), inbox placement prediction, up to 3 deliverability issues, and suggested revisions you can apply instantly.
