Lead Pipeline

From First Touch to Renewal

A five-stage pipeline tracks every prospect: Leads, Qualified (took a test), Active Opportunities (attended sessions), Customers (purchased), and Renewals. Know exactly where everyone stands.

  • Lead Scoring

    Scores are calculated from recency, engagement level, communication history, and payment method on file — so you always know who to prioritize.

  • Source Analytics & Revenue Attribution

    Track where your leads come from and attribute revenue to specific channels, so you can double down on what works.

  • Open Carts Recovery

    View abandoned carts in real time and take action to recover lost revenue before prospects move on.
