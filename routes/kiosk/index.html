Student Check-In

Tap a Session. Tap Your Name. Done.

A tablet in your lobby shows today's sessions at that location, with each tutor and student. Students check themselves in, and staff see who has arrived without anyone at the desk.

  • Today's Schedule, Live

    Every session at the location appears with its time, tutor, and students, and updates as the day changes.

  • Two-Tap Check-In

    Students tap their session, then their name. Each session shows how many students have checked in.

  • Photos Tutors Recognize

    Students without a profile photo are invited to add one at check-in, so tutors know who they're meeting.

  • Need Help? Talk to the Team

    One button connects the lobby to your staff when a family has a question.

Time Clock

Clock In With a Photo, Not a Paper Sheet

Staff clock in and out from the Staff Portal or the lobby kiosk. Clocking in from the portal takes a quick photo, so you know who actually clocked in.

  • Photo-Verified Punches

    Clocking in and out from the portal captures a quick photo of whoever is punching.

  • Face Match or One-Time Code

    On the lobby kiosk, staff clock in by face match after a one-time enrollment, or with a one-time code.

  • Hours That Add Themselves Up

    Each staff member sees hours this week, their average start time, and their on-time rate.

  • Contact Hours Alongside

    Teaching and contact hours sit next to clocked time in the same portal view.

Any Tablet Becomes a Kiosk in a Minute

Open the kiosk page on a tablet and it shows a six-digit code. A manager enters the code, picks the location, and the tablet starts showing that location's schedule.

  • Pair With a Code

    No accounts to log in on the tablet. Pairing is done from a manager's dashboard.

  • One Kiosk Per Location

    Each kiosk shows only its own location's sessions and staff.
