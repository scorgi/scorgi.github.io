Problem Lifecycle

8 Stages from Draft to Production

Every problem moves through a structured lifecycle that enforces quality at each gate. Once a problem accumulates 10 or more student responses, it becomes Immutable — locked from further edits to protect data integrity.

  • Incomplete

    Problem created but missing required fields. Cannot be used in tests.

  • Gradable → Completed

    Answer key is set (Gradable), then all content fields are filled (Completed). Ready for review.

  • In Review → Accepted

    Enters QA with 2+ reviewers. Consensus required to advance to Accepted status.

  • Immutable (QA Safeguard)

    After 10+ student responses, the problem is permanently locked. No edits allowed — protecting the integrity of existing score data and analytics.

  • Deprecated

    Retired from active use but preserved for historical reporting and data continuity.
