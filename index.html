For Educators

Built for Tutoring Companies That Want to Scale

Management Dashboard

Complete business visibility in one view. Action logs track every platform event. A 5-stage lead pipeline (Leads → Qualified → Active → Customers → Renewals) with automatic lead scoring prioritizes your hottest prospects. See abandoned carts, failed transactions, and revenue attribution by marketing channel.

  • Lead scoring and conversion tracking
  • Session approval queue with payment status
  • Revenue attribution by lead source
  • Contact list export for targeted outreach

Staff Scheduling

Calendar or Table view with 5 operating modes — View, Add, Edit, Move (drag-to-reschedule), and Cancel. Filter by date range, location, and staff member. Advanced tools include availability calculators, session demand heatmaps, and Selectat for bulk-proposing recurring schedules.

Content Creation Platform

Five interconnected tools for creating, reviewing, and publishing test content. Problems follow an 8-stage lifecycle with a multi-reviewer QA system requiring 2+ consensus. Kanban-style production management tracks every problem from draft to deployment.

  • Problem Editor with metadata, solutions, and rationales
  • Test Editor with timed sections and scoring configs
  • QA Tool with 11 issue categories and reviewer consensus
  • Shared HTML Editor with MathJax and CDN media
For Students & Families

Your Child's Success, Powered by Technology

Insights & Worksheet Generator

Topic-level skill tiers (Beginner → Mastery) calculated from accuracy, speed, and difficulty. Trend indicators show Improving, Declining, or Stable. Staleness warnings flag topics unpracticed for 14+ days. Speed risk alerts detect when a student is 30%+ slower than peers.

Worksheet Generator — Select struggling topics, set quantity (max 30 problems), and generate targeted practice from the full problem bank. Group mode creates shared worksheets for up to 8 students.

School Tracker

Browse thousands of schools with fuzzy search. Filter by acceptance rate, tuition, SAT/ACT fit, and distance from zip code. Our fit analysis algorithm classifies each school as Likely, Match, or Reach based on the student's best scores.

  • 8 application statuses with deadlines and checklists
  • Drag-and-drop podium rankings with trophies
  • 4-year cost estimator with in-state/out-of-state toggle
  • Secure share tokens for parents and counselors
